# Eden Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 10 September 2026

This policy explains how Eden handles information when you use the Eden iOS application.

## Who is responsible for the app?

The controller of the information described in this policy is **Seweryn Michalak**, located in **Poland**, contactable at [eden.app.feedback@gmail.com](mailto:eden.app.feedback@gmail.com).

## Information stored by Eden

Eden is designed to work primarily on your device. Depending on the features you use, the app may store locally:

- quiz progress, answers, scores and XP;
- history, levels, badges, treasures and remembered thoughts you enter;
- language, appearance, notification and quiz settings;
- data needed to display the Eden widget;
- local diagnostic events used to help investigate a problem.

The app does not require an account and does not collect your quiz answers or private notes on a remote server.

## Diagnostic information

When you contact us through the feedback form, you can choose whether to include diagnostic information. If you include it, the message may contain the app version, iOS version, device model, language, a session identifier and a short list of technical events such as opening a screen or submitting a quiz answer.

Diagnostic information is designed not to include the content of your answers, remembered thoughts or daily-text material. It is sent only when you choose to send the email and is delivered through your email provider.

## Notifications and widget

If you enable notifications, Eden schedules local notifications on your device. The widget reads the minimum shared local data needed to show your progress. You can disable notifications in Eden or in iOS Settings. Removing the app removes its local data and widget data stored by the app.

## External links

Eden can open links to the JW Library application or the JW.ORG/WOL website. When you follow such a link, the selected destination may process information under its own privacy policy. Eden does not control those services.

## Sharing and disclosure

Eden does not sell your information and does not use it for advertising. We do not share local app data with third parties unless you explicitly include diagnostic information in a feedback email, or disclosure is required by law.

Apple may process information according to Apple's own policies when you download, update or use system services. See Apple's privacy information at <https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/>.

## Retention and deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you remove it using the app's available reset option or delete Eden. Diagnostic information included in an email is retained in the feedback mailbox for as long as reasonably necessary to respond and maintain the app, then deleted according to the mailbox owner's retention practices.

To ask about a diagnostic email or request deletion of information you sent to us, contact [eden.app.feedback@gmail.com](mailto:eden.app.feedback@gmail.com).

## Children's privacy

Eden is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. Because Eden does not require an account or collect quiz content remotely, use of the app remains primarily local to the device.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The effective date above will be updated and the current version will remain available at the public policy URL.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to [eden.app.feedback@gmail.com](mailto:eden.app.feedback@gmail.com).
